Garfin

Design pack v1 · everything GPLv3-safe

One fish, one job: hand-pick what the kids can watch. The identity is warm and slightly silly on the surface, precise underneath — the same balance the app itself needs, since it's a toy-looking thing that writes real permissions to a real server.

01 — Wordmark

Five proposals

All set in Fredoka SemiBold, lowercase. Lowercase matters: it keeps the name friendly and stops it reading as an acronym or a holding company. Pick one and I'll cut the final vector.

garfin

1 · Plain

Fredoka as drawn, tracking tightened to −0.5. Nothing clever. Ages best, survives any size, and never fights the mark sitting next to it.

garfn

2 · Eye-dot my pick

The tittle on the i becomes the fish's eye. One tiny move ties the wordmark to the mark, so the word works alone without losing the character.

garfin

3 · Tail

A caudal fin flicks off the final n, so the word swims. Charming at banner size, mush below 20px — pair it with a plain small-size variant.

garfin

4 · Bubbles

Three bubbles rise off the end. The most obviously kid-facing option, and the easiest to animate for a splash screen. Risks reading as a bath-toy brand.

garfin

5 · Sticker

Enclosed in a pill, like something you'd stick on a lunchbox. Excellent as a README badge or a repo avatar; too loud as a page header.

How to choose

Pick 2 (Eye-dot) if you want one wordmark that does everything. It's the only option here that carries brand character at 16px.

Or run 1 (Plain) as the workhorse and keep 5 (Sticker) for the GitHub avatar and merch. That's a normal split and costs nothing.

Minimum sizes

04 — Typography

Fredoka and Nunito

Both under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, which the FSF lists as free and GPL-compatible. Ship OFL.txt beside the .ttf files and register them in LicenseRegistry. Deliberately not Roboto: it's Apache 2.0, which is fine with GPLv3 but incompatible with GPLv2, and that would close off relicensing later.

Fredoka SemiBold

Headlines, screen titles, numbers, buttons, avatars, the wordmark. Rounded terminals do the friendliness so the copy doesn't have to.

Regular 400 · Medium 500 · SemiBold 600 · Bold 700

Nunito

Body, list rows, settings, everything you actually read. Rounded enough to sit with Fredoka, neutral enough to disappear at 14px.

07 — Voice

Plain, warm, never cute about permissions

Do

  • Say what happens: "Paddington is on Emma's shelf"
  • Use the children's names — the app knows them
  • Keep verbs concrete: pick, hand, share, take back
  • Be plain about the licence and the lack of affiliation

Don't

  • Call it "safe", "protected", or "secure" — it's a shortlist, not a guarantee
  • Use surveillance words: monitor, track, watch over, control
  • Be jokey in errors, warnings, or anything about ratings
  • Imply Jellyfin endorses it

Boilerplate

One line: Hand-pick what your kids can watch.

Repo description: Hand-pick what your kids can watch. An Android app for managing Jellyfin parental controls through tags.

Required note, kept visible: Garfin is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the Jellyfin project. Jellyfin is a trademark of Jellyfin, Inc.

08 — Licensing the identity

Two different things, two different terms

Code is GPL-3.0. Brand assets are not code, and copyleft on a logo produces an odd result: anyone could ship a modified app under your mark. The usual free-software answer is to license the artwork permissively for use while reserving the name and mark as an identifier.

Suggested wording for BRANDING.md: The Garfin name and fish mark identify this project. The artwork is CC BY-SA 4.0 — use it freely to write about, link to, or package Garfin. If you fork and distribute a modified version, please give it another name and another mark, so nobody downloads yours thinking it is this one.
